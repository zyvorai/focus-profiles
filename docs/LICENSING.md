# Licence and trial

Focus Profiles is a paid app with a 30-day trial. **Licensing is soft: the app never disables a feature.**

## Without a key

Everything works. The menu and the Licence window show a status line:

> Unlicensed: everything works, no licence key yet

## Get a trial key

Write to **sales@zyvor.dev** and ask for a 30-day trial key. Choose **Licence…** from the menu-bar menu, paste the key (it looks like `ZYV1.…`) and press **Verify and Save**. The key is checked on your Mac; the Licence window states that nothing is sent anywhere.

While a trial or paid key is active, the status line shows the days left, for example `Trial: 12 days left`. After a key expires it shows `Licence expired on <date>`. Nothing is switched off in any case.

## Remove a key

**Licence…** > **Remove Key…**. The app keeps working and shows as unlicensed.
